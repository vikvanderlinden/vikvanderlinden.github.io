I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the [DistriNet research unit](https://distrinet.cs.kuleuven.be/) at the [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/kuleuven/index.html), working under the supervision of [Mathy Vanhoef](https://www.mathyvanhoef.com/) and [Tom Van Goethem](https://tom.vg/).

My research focus is in web and network security, and I'm part of the [Web Security and Privacy team](#web-security-and-privacy). I mainly focus on web-based side-channels, both on application- and network protocol level, specifically timing attacks.

Besides my research, I manage [Stellar Vector](https://stellarvector.be/), the CTF team powered by DistriNet and KU Leuven. Please feel free to join our next event if you enjoy the occasional h4cking!

I'm active as TA in following courses:

* [G0Q30EN](https://onderwijsaanbod.kuleuven.be/2023/syllabi/n/G0Q30EN.htm) Informaticawerktuigen
* [H04K5AE](https://onderwijsaanbod.kuleuven.be/2023/syllabi/e/H04K5AE.htm) Development of Secure Software
